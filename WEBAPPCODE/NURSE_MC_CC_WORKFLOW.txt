SIMPLIFIED PARENT + NURSE MC/CC WORKFLOW

Parent side:
- Parents only fill up basic maternal and infant information.
- Parents can request schedules and view reminders.
- Parents do not fill the full MC/CC template fields.

Nurse/Midwife side:
- Nurse/Midwife sees only records from their assigned barangay.
- The dashboard shows an MC/CC Completion Queue.
- Maternal records have a Complete MC button.
- Infant records have a Complete CC button.
- The nurse/midwife completes the clinical/template fields used for monthly reports.

Monthly Reports:
- MC = Maternal Care monthly report.
- CC = Child Immunization monthly report.
- Reports are generated automatically from the records already stored in the system.
- Nurse/Midwife accounts can generate MC/CC only for their assigned barangay.
- Clicking Auto-generate MC/CC creates or updates both reports for the selected month.
- The report count values are not manually typed; they come from maternal, infant, schedule, and nurse-completed MC/CC data.

Supabase:
- If you already ran update-mc-cc-template-fields.sql before, no new SQL is needed.
- The system stores detailed MC/CC answers inside formDetails JSONB.
