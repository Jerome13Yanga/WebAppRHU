HEALTHCARE STAFF & PARENT REGISTRATION SETUP

1. Healthcare Staff Registration:
   - Healthcare personnel (Nurse / Midwife and MHO) register directly using the "Healthcare Staff Registration" form on the login screen.
   - Staff provide their Name, Professional Email, Role Assignment, Barangay Assignment, and Password.
   - Accounts are created securely via Supabase Auth and saved to the profiles/users directory.

2. Mother / Parent Registration:
   - Parents register via the "Mother / Parent Registration" form on the login screen.

3. Administrator Role:
   - Administrators (admin@rhu.gov) oversee central clinical monitoring, database backup/recovery, and monthly DOH reporting.
   - User account creation and authentication are handled self-service via the respective login screen registration forms.
